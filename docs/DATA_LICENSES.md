# Data and Source Licenses

Tender separates software licensing from data licensing. Original Tender code
and documentation use the PolyForm Noncommercial License 1.0.0. That license
does not override third-party source terms; commercial reuse of any included
material requires separate legal clearance and written permission where applicable.

## Knowledge data

- Chinese high-school curriculum structure: organized from public Ministry of
  Education curriculum standards. The repository stores a structured index,
  not scanned copies of the standards.
- Undergraduate major catalogue: based on public Ministry of Education
  catalogues.
- Course seeds: representative course names and ordering for a subset of
  majors. They are not official university培养方案.
- O*NET 30.1: career titles, interest mappings and keywords. O*NET is developed
  by the U.S. Department of Labor and licensed under CC BY 4.0.
- Learner evidence database in this repository is empty. No real learner data
  is distributed.

## Excluded material

- Real tenant databases;
- Real learner conversations;
- Guardian and identity records;
- Private institutional reports;
- Source PDFs whose redistribution terms are unclear;
- Copyleft or non-commercial datasets that are not compatible with this
  distribution.

## Upstream research

Public benchmarks and research datasets are cited in reports rather than
redistributed unless their licenses permit redistribution. Users are
responsible for checking current upstream terms before reusing derived data.

## Attribution

When redistributing derived datasets, retain source names, retrieval dates,
license notes and transformations. Do not describe a course seed as an official
university curriculum, and do not describe heuristic bridges as official
subject-selection requirements.
