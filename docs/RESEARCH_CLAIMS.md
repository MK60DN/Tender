# Research Claims and Limits

## Supported engineering claims

- The application starts and serves the documented APIs.
- DAOQL stores events, causal edges and an audit hash chain.
- Language signals preserve source spans, confidence and alternative explanations.
- The knowledge forest is generated from the included databases.
- The regression suite covers API contracts, authorization, safety routing,
  knowledge retrieval, language evidence and core state modeling.

## Preliminary research claims

- Predictive-processing language is used as a modeling framework.
- Retrieval, spacing and metacognitive calibration motivate the interaction design.
- The Tender benchmark contains useful signal but is not superior across all
  metrics; logistic regression is stronger on the published global comparison.

## Not claimed

- Clinical diagnosis or detection;
- Direct mind reading;
- Guaranteed learning gains;
- Completed real-world causal evidence for the full system;
- Official subject-selection, admission or employment decisions;
- Universal superiority over BKT, DKT, logistic regression or other baselines.

## Required reporting

Any future study should report pre-registration, sample provenance, missing data,
uncertainty, calibration, negative outcomes and conflicts of interest.
